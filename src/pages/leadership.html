How we operate

Governance

WINGS-AAS is registered as a cooperative society and operates under democratic member governance — every member has a voice in shaping the cooperative's direction.

  • Democratic member control through general body meetings.
  • An elected board accountable to the membership.
  • Transparent reporting of activities and finances.
  • Advisory support from patrons, scientists, and policy experts.
