About WINGS

Building Leaders. Strengthening Communities. Transforming Agriculture.

Our story

Women are the backbone of agriculture, yet they often remain underserved in access to knowledge, technology, finance, markets, and leadership opportunities.

WINGS-AAS was established to change this reality.

As a dynamic women-led cooperative, WINGS creates an enabling ecosystem where women can learn, innovate, collaborate, lead, and succeed. We connect agriculture with science, research with industry, and local communities with national and global opportunities.

Through strategic partnerships and collective action, WINGS supports sustainable livelihoods, women-led enterprises, climate-resilient agriculture, and inclusive rural development.

Our direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build an inclusive agricultural ecosystem where every woman has the opportunity, knowledge, confidence, and resources to lead sustainable change.

Our Mission

  • Empower women through education, leadership, and capacity building.
  • Promote entrepreneurship and women-led agricultural enterprises.
  • Strengthen access to technology, finance, markets, and government initiatives.
  • Foster innovation through research, incubation, and collaboration.
  • Develop resilient value chains and market opportunities.
  • Create safe, inclusive, and gender-responsive agricultural workplaces.
