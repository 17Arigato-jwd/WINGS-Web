Our Focus

Eight interconnected areas where WINGS creates lasting impact.

Focus area 01

Women Empowerment

We create pathways for women in agriculture to access knowledge, finance, leadership roles, and decision-making platforms. From grassroots self-confidence building to national-level representation, empowerment runs through everything WINGS does.

Focus area 02

Sustainable Agriculture

We promote climate-resilient, environmentally responsible farming practices that protect soil, water, and biodiversity while remaining economically viable — helping communities farm for today without compromising tomorrow.

Focus area 03

Entrepreneurship & Startups

We nurture women-led agricultural enterprises and agri-startups through incubation, mentorship, and access to funding — turning ideas into businesses that create jobs and strengthen rural economies.

Explore Membership

Focus area 04

Innovation & Technology

We bridge research and the field — connecting scientists, institutions, and industry so modern tools, digital agriculture, and new techniques reach the women who can put them to work.

Focus area 05

Capacity Building & Skill Development

Training programs, certifications, workshops, and mentorship that build practical skills and leadership capability — for members at every stage, from first job to boardroom.

Focus area 06

Partnerships & Collaborations

We work with academic institutions, industry, government, and development organizations — because collective action moves further and faster than any single organization can.

Our Partners

Focus area 07

Market Linkages & Value Chains

We help women producers reach markets on fair terms — building resilient value chains, aggregation, and buyer connections that turn good harvests into good incomes.

Focus area 08

Community Development

Stronger livelihoods, inclusive growth, and resilient rural communities — the outcomes that all our other focus areas ultimately serve.
